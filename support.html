The calculator

How do percentages work?

Like a desk calculator: 200 + 10% adds ten percent of 200, giving 220, and 80 − 25% gives 60. Anywhere else a percentage is a fraction: 50 × 10% is 5. The working shows which reading was used (“10% of 200 = 20”).

Why does 0.1 + 0.2 show 0.3?

Because it is 0.3. The app removes the tiny rounding noise many calculators show. Where an answer is an exact fraction it is shown too — 1/3 + 1/6 also shows ½. You can turn fractions off in Settings.

How do I see the working?

After you press =, tap Steps to see every operation, one line at a time. Tap Explain to have the on-device AI describe the working in plain words as well — the numbers always come from the calculator.

Where are the scientific keys?

Tap the grid button at the top of the calculator. You get trig and inverse trig (tap 2nd), hyperbolic functions, ln, log and log₂, powers and roots, factorial, nCr and nPr, abs, mod, π, e and EE. Switch between degrees and radians with the DEG / RAD button, or in Settings.

Memory, Ans, undo and redo

M+ and M− add the current answer to memory or take it away, MR recalls it and MC clears it. Ans is the last answer. The undo and redo arrows in the display step back and forward through what you typed; press and hold ⌫ to clear the line.

It says “too large to show”

The answer is beyond what any calculator can hold (about 1.8 × 10³⁰⁸) — for example 200!. Answers up to that size are shown in scientific notation, like 170! = 7.2574156153E306.

How do I change how numbers look?

Settings → Number format: Automatic (up to 12 significant figures), Fixed decimals (0–10 places) or Scientific, with or without thousands separators. Money answers show two decimal places in Automatic.

Ask

What can I type?

Plain English. For example: “30 is what percent of 120”, “18% tip on $64 split 3 ways”, “25% off 80”, “100 plus 8% tax”, “compound interest on 1000 at 5% for 10 years”, “mortgage payment on 200000 at 6% for 30 years”, “convert 5 km to miles”, “how many days between 2026-01-01 and 2026-12-25”, “solve x^2 − 5x + 6 = 0”, “x + y = 10, x − y = 2”, “average of 3, 5, 10”, “is 97 prime”, “255 in hex”, or set a variable with “rate = 0.07”. These are answered by the calculator itself, with no AI.

What does the AI do?

For word problems the calculator can’t read by itself, the on-device AI rewrites the words as a sum. It never does the arithmetic. The calculator checks every number in that sum came from your question, compares more than one reading, then works it out. “Understood as …” above the answer shows exactly what was calculated.

I see a warning under an answer

Something was uncertain — the readings didn’t agree, or a number appeared that wasn’t in your question. Check the “Understood as …” line; if it isn’t what you meant, rephrase the question or type the sum on the keypad.

The AI explained something wrongly

It is a small model and it can be wrong. Explanations are marked as AI explanations, and any sum written in them — like “2 + 2 = 5” — is checked and corrected on screen. Answers to calculations always come from the calculator, never from the model.

How do I put the keyboard away?

Tap anywhere in the conversation or on the Ask title, or drag the conversation down. Tapping the question box keeps it open.

Why is the first AI answer slow?

The model loads the first time the AI is needed after the app opens — a few seconds, longer on older devices. After that, answers start straight away. Plain-English questions the calculator can read itself never wait for the model.

The AI couldn’t be loaded

Close other apps you aren’t using and try again — the model needs a fair amount of free memory. The calculator, the plain-English answers and every tool keep working without it. You can also switch the AI off in Settings.

Tools

What tools are there?

Unit converter (13 categories, 90+ units), percentages, tip & split, discount & tax & margin, loan & mortgage with a yearly schedule, savings & growth (compound interest, monthly deposits, growth rate), statistics with a histogram, equation solver (one equation or simultaneous), graph plotter, programmer (decimal, hex, binary, octal and bitwise), dates, fractions, primes & factors, best buy, and memory & variables.

Can I type a sum into a field?

Yes. Every number field accepts arithmetic — type 2*32 and it uses 64. You can use your own variables too, like rate*100.

How does the graph plotter work?

Enter f(x), and optionally g(x). The plotter marks where each curve crosses zero and where the two curves meet; drag across the graph to trace values. Choose radians or degrees for trigonometry.

Can it convert currencies?

No. Exchange rates change constantly and the app never goes online, so it has no live rates. Every other converter works fully offline.

History

What is saved?

Every finished sum, every Ask answer, and any tool result you save — grouped by day. Search it, filter by pinned, keypad, Ask or tools, pin and label entries, and tap one to open it, reuse it in the calculator, copy or share it.

I deleted something by mistake

Tap Undo in the bar that appears right after a delete, or use the undo and redo arrows at the top of History. They cover every deletion, clear, pin and label change until the app is closed.

How do I delete several at once?

History → ⋯ → Select, tick the entries, then Delete. Swipe left on a single entry to delete it, or right to pin it. ⋯ → Clear history removes everything, or everything except pinned entries.

How long is history kept?

Choose in Settings: forever, 1 year, 90, 30 or 7 days. Pinned entries are always kept. You can also turn history saving off.

How do I back it up?

History → ⋯ → Share as text or Export CSV (spreadsheet), or Settings → Export history. Save or send the file wherever you like — the app has no sync of its own.

iPad and smaller iPhones

iPad

The sections are in a rail down the left. In portrait the calculator shows a paper-tape of your recent sums — tap one to reuse it. In landscape, your full history sits beside the keys.

iPhone SE and other compact iPhones

With scientific keys on, the function keys are on two pages: swipe across them, or tap the small gold page marker, to switch.

The app

Why is it so large?

About 780 MB installed — the AI model built into the app is about 770 MB of that. Nothing more is ever downloaded.

How do I change the look?

Settings → Appearance: Espresso (satin espresso brown with gold) or Cream (satin cream with gold and espresso).

Does it cost anything or need an account?

No. It is free, with no in-app purchases, no ads, no account, no sign-in and no subscription.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. The AI runs on the device itself, so newer devices answer faster.
